Fine Art Walls Canvas adWalls

Untitled

Lavagna, Liguria, Italy — 2020

Festival: Festivart

Technique: Spray paint on wall

Painted for the Festivart festival in Lavagna, this piece renders a surreal bird as a glowing anatomical study: a golden beak and red crest give way to an exposed neon spine and translucent, electrified wing membranes. Set against a black background inside a sketched frame, the composition pushes the artist's signature glow-effects and light-emission spray techniques to full intensity.
