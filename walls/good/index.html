Fine Art Walls Canvas adWalls

Good

Imola, Italy — 2017

Festival: RestArt - Urban Festival

Technique: Spray paint on wall

Painted with Moe and Korvo for the RestArt Urban Festival in Imola, this collaborative wall gathers symbols of fortune and serenity around a laughing golden Buddha, who cradles a glowing heart while riding a floating manta ray. A surreal long-beaked bird, a monocled golden bee and a glowing four-leaf clover complete the scene, set against wildstyle lettering on a dark background.
