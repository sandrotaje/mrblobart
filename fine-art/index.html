THE ARTIST

MR. BLOB — Contemporary Urban Artist & Sculptor. Active on the international urban art scene, Mr Blob bridges the gap between raw street energy and the refined aesthetics of fine art. His work explores the interaction between materials, shapes, and urban spaces, featuring vivid characters and tangible textures that challenge the boundaries of contemporary public art.
